GOOD NIGHT ELISA — narrator script (voice: Cruella, ElevenLabs)
One file per line, MP3, named exactly as below. Send them back and they drop straight into the game.

wake1.mp3
This is the story of a little girl named Elisa. Every night, she woke up in the dark. And every night, she was very, very brave. Or so she told herself.

wake2.mp3
And once again, Elisa woke up. Some children never learn.

nudge.mp3
Elisa could, of course, simply get up. Any time now, darling.

getup.mp3
Elisa climbed out of bed, determined to find her parents.

wardrobe.mp3
The wardrobe door opened. By itself. Naturally.

idle.mp3
Elisa stood perfectly still, waiting, perhaps, for the dark to get bored.

bed.mp3
Elisa considered going back to bed. A tempting idea. Also a cowardly one.

drawer.mp3
In the desk drawer, Elisa found her flashlight. How wonderfully convenient.

nobatt.mp3
It did not turn on. Of course it didn't. Nothing is ever that easy.

batt1.mp3
One battery. Elisa needed two. Mathematics can be so cruel.

batt2.mp3
And the second battery. Light, at last.

dolls.mp3
The dolls on the shelf watched Elisa. They always did.

chest.mp3
The toy chest. Full of old friends. Most of them harmless.

window.mp3
Outside, the garden. And the doghouse. Whatever had been chained there was no longer chained there.

drawing.mp3
A drawing. Elisa didn't remember drawing it. That was the interesting part.

bunny.mp3
Bunny was asleep. Bunny was always asleep when Elisa needed him.

eyes_wardrobe.mp3
Something in the wardrobe was looking at Elisa. Elisa chose not to look back. Wise.

eyes_bed.mp3
Then, under the bed. Two eyes. Patient ones.

coats.mp3
Coats. Just coats. How very disappointing for everyone.

toys.mp3
Toys. Only toys. Elisa almost sounded relieved.

hall_dark.mp3
Elisa stepped into the dark hallway without a light. Bold. Foolish, but bold.

hall_lamp.mp3
Flashlight in hand, Elisa stepped into the hallway. And the story could finally begin.

end_scare.mp3
And that, I'm afraid, was the end of Elisa. For tonight.

end_paralysis.mp3
Elisa did not move. The night moved for her.

unreachable.mp3
Elisa could not reach that. Elisa is small. We've been over this.

again.mp3
Elisa looked again. Nothing had changed. Things rarely do.
